What stands out

Three findings worth taking into your presentation.

AdoptionUPI + QR dominate

89.4% selected UPI or QR Code as their main digital payment method in this sample.

ReliabilityFailures are common

89.4% reported experiencing failed digital payments at least rarely, with poor internet the most frequent issue.

TrustSecurity needs attention

62.8% are concerned or very concerned about digital payment security, while 43.4% encountered suspicious calls, SMS or links.


Research note: the sample is student-heavy and concentrated among younger respondents. Treat these percentages as findings from this survey sample rather than as a population-wide estimate.
